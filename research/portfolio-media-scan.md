Treat it as a requirements scrapbook, not an implementation shortcut. None of these historical claims was independently runtime-verified in this scan.

## Adjacent ideas worth testing

1. **Recording passport:** one portable JSON receipt links ingest copies, joined segments, original recording, derivatives and QC findings. Start with MediaIngest reports + Conjoyn/P2/Avid grouping + VAM import. A small interchange feature across existing apps may be more useful than another app.
2. **MediaCheck evidence pack:** each issue gets timestamp, still frame, technical evidence and a concise HTML/JSON report. Combines the integrity engine, Stills From Video and existing audit exporters. Keep it a MediaCheck feature until sharing demand is demonstrated.
3. **Archive reconnect review:** connect a drive and preview renamed/missing/changed recordings before applying catalog updates. Builds on VAM inode/volume resolution; cross-volume identity and NAS behavior require distinct proof.
4. **Recording-set preflight:** show whether split files, mono stems, sidecars and timecode form a complete, compatible recording before ingest/join/export. Combines Conjoyn guards, P2 XML, Avid aggregation; initially a read-only checklist inside existing tools.
5. **Personal location-sound notebook:** extract a selected sound with source video/time range, location and tags; audition later without finding the full shoot. A narrow SoundHarvest prototype using Penumbra interaction patterns and VAM metadata, with high-resolution PCM preservation explicitly tested.

These are portfolio-based hypotheses, not validated market opportunities. Best next experiment: choose a handful of real problem recordings and compare the existing apps’ outputs with one small candidate workflow before expanding product scope.
