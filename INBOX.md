# Idea inbox

Capture rough ideas here before deciding whether to explore them. A sentence or two is enough; no spec is required.

For each entry, note the date, the problem or observation, who experiences it, and any example or link. When it develops, move the detail into `ideas/<name>/README.md` using the [template](ideas/TEMPLATE.md), then add it to the [index](README.md#candidate-ideas).
